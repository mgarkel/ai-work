# Rules for kinds of work

The plan says *what* to make; these rules say how to make that kind of thing well. Work out which kind (or kinds) the job is, apply the rules, and list them in the plan.

**The user's rules win.** If the user's `ai-work-rules.md` has rules for this kind of work, use theirs; they add to or replace these.

## Starter set

**A message** (an email, an update, an announcement)

- Put the key point first: what you need or what happened, in the first two lines.
- Match the tone to the reader and the relationship.
- End with a clear ask: who should do what, by when.
- Keep only what the reader needs; move background to an attachment or a link.

**A decision** (choosing between options)

- Lay out the realistic options, each with its tradeoffs.
- Give a recommendation, with the reasons tied to the goal.
- State the assumptions the recommendation rests on, and what would change it.
- Say what happens next once it's decided.

**An analysis** (research, numbers, a comparison)

- Open with a short summary of the findings and what they mean.
- Keep what the data shows separate from what it might mean.
- Say how sure you are about each finding, and why.
- Add a "so what" to each finding: why it matters for the goal.
- Say where the data came from, the time period, and any gaps.

**A meeting** (an agenda, preparation, follow-up)

- Give each agenda item an objective and a time.
- Prepare the questions to ask and the decisions to reach.
- Think ahead about where the discussion might go and what's sensitive.
- For follow-ups: decisions, owners, and dates.

## Any other kind of work

The list isn't complete. For a kind of work not listed (a grant application, a policy, a lesson plan, a proposal, a report), work out what good looks like for that kind: how experienced people in that field do it, and what readers of it expect. Write three or four rules like the ones above into the plan, under "Rules for this kind of work", where the user can see and change them. The debrief can offer to save them to the user's rules as a new kind of work.
