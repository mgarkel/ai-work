# Credits

ai-work stands on the shoulders of others. Apart from discernment-nudge, which is included unchanged under its own license, everything here is written in our own words and released under the MIT License; the ideas come from these sources.

## Included

- **[discernment-nudge](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/discernment-nudge)** by Anthropic, Apache License 2.0 (see `skills/discernment-nudge/LICENSE.txt`). Copied unchanged from commit `f379e5ad66e2febc1616cf8d6284666fecbe514e`.

## Ideas adapted

- **[grill-me](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills)** (Matt Pocock): questions in rounds, each with a recommended answer; confirming shared understanding before acting.
- **[CoworkPowers](https://github.com/nabeelhyatt/coworkpowers)** (Nabeel Hyatt): sizing effort to stakes; must-ask questions for important work; approval options; the devil's advocate; severity levels; lesson types; rules for kinds of work; checking alignment while working.
- **[doc-coauthoring](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/doc-coauthoring)** (Anthropic): reader testing with a fresh reader who gets only the document; the author owns the final read.
- **[Compound Knowledge](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-knowledge-plugin)** (Every): the plan as a running log; handling blockers; the confidence check; a few specific, approved lessons; checking for outdated lessons.
- **[Compound Engineering](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin)** (Every): approaches before a recommendation; testing whether it's the real problem; not reopening settled decisions; mapping unfamiliar choices first; plan reviewers; evidence-based findings; fixing clear issues and asking about judgment calls.
- **Established practice:** the creative brief and project charter; the pre-mortem (Gary Klein); checklists (Atul Gawande); independent review and red teams; the after-action review (US Army).
- **Vendor guidance:** Anthropic's ["How people check Claude's work"](https://academy.claude.com/tutorials/discernment-toolkit) and OpenAI Academy's guidance on planning and checking work.
